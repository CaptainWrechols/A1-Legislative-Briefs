# Appendix H — Inside the budget trailer (HB2)

New Hampshire makes much of its energy law inside HB2, the omnibus policy bill that accompanies each two-year budget. Every extracted section of the last three trailers was screened against the energy terms and hand-reviewed; this appendix lists the sections kept (three rate sections the term matcher missed were added by hand and flagged in the working files). **All votes on HB2 are on the whole trailer, never on one section.**
