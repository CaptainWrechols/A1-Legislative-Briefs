# Appendix A — Bills overview

One row per bill in the curated set (189 bills, hand-reviewed from 5,548 keyword hits plus four universe additions). *Tier*: Core bills are the headline set for the six citizen proposals plus the ethics/open-government accountability agenda; Adjacent bills are the same lanes but narrower, procedural, or federal-target; Context bills are kept for audit only. *Result*: what finally happened — 'Adopted (resolution)' means both chambers adopted a measure that never goes to the governor. Lead sponsor is the first member on the official sponsor line; party labels are intentionally not shown (see Appendix F).
