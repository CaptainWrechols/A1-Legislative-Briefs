# Appendix F — What this data cannot say

Plain-language limits of the dataset behind the brief. None of these limits
is bridged with estimates anywhere in the packet.

1. **The set is curated, not exhaustive.** Bills were discovered by keyword
   search of official full text plus a title/summary scan of the certified
   statewide universe, then hand-reviewed from 5,548 hits down to 189. Four
   REACH Act civics bills were hand-added from the certified universe
   because their text contains none of the issue's search terms — evidence
   that keyword discovery can miss relevant bills whose language differs.
   The exclusion rules are documented in the curation map.

2. **No committee vote counts exist.** South Carolina publishes committee
   *outcomes* ("Favorable," "Favorable with amendment") but never tally
   tables. When this packet says a bill "died in committee," there is no
   recorded vote behind that stop — usually no recorded action at all — and
   no committee tally may be inferred or implied.

3. **No party labels.** Sponsor and vote party breakdowns require a roster
   join that was not fetched for this brief. The packet therefore makes no
   claims about which party supported or blocked anything. Phrases like
   "leadership of both parties" describe members' chamber leadership roles,
   not a party-line vote analysis.

4. **Vote counts are verbatim and passage-type only.** Floor tallies come
   directly from the chamber vote-history tables. Procedural motions —
   tabling amendments, motions to continue, rule waivers — are excluded
   from support signals. Two procedural votes are described in the brief
   because they decided outcomes, and they are labeled as what they are:
   the Senate's 26–18 motion to continue H5683 (a shelving motion, not a
   passage vote) and the House's 23–86 refusal to concur in Senate
   amendments to H3570 (a version dispute, not a vote against the House's
   own 102–0 version).

5. **Resolutions are not laws.** Article V applications (H3008, H3007)
   travel as concurrent resolutions: adopted by both chambers, never signed
   by the governor, and creating no state law. "Adopted" in this packet
   means exactly that and nothing more.

6. **"Did not pass" for the 126th session is as of the collection date
   (2026-08-26),** after the session's final regular calendar. Later
   special-session action, if any, is not in this data.

7. **The FY 2020-21 budget cycle has no enacted Part IB proviso set** (the
   COVID continuing-resolution year), so budget-proviso coverage runs
   FY 2021-22 through FY 2026-27.

8. **Why bills died is not in the data.** The record shows where each bill
   stopped, never the reasoning. Motives, deals, and informal opposition
   are invisible here, and the packet does not guess at them.

9. **The six constituent proposals are Forum process input** from the
   Phase 2 Community Conversations (June–August 2026), labeled [P-…]. They
   record what participants proposed and how often — they are not verified
   facts, polling, or positions of The Forum.
