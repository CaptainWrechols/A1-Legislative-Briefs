# Appendix G — Ethics and elections provisos in the state budget

South Carolina enacts one-year policy rules — provisos — inside Part IB of
each annual General Appropriations Act. A proviso lasts one budget year
unless re-adopted, and floor votes attach to the whole budget bill (or a
named amendment), never to a single proviso. The table below is the
hand-curated set of provisos that bear on ethics enforcement, election-law
power, lobbying restrictions, and civic education, selected from the full
term-matched candidate sets (191–234 provisos per cycle). FY 2020-21 is a
gap: the budget bill (H5201) died in committee during COVID and the state
ran on a continuing resolution, so that year has no enacted Part IB.

## The recurring machinery

| Proviso (by caption) | Years present | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| GP: Actions on Election Law | every cycle since FY 2021-22 | Gives the Senate President and House Speaker an unconditional right to intervene in any lawsuit challenging an election law or how an election is run. This litigation power exists only as a one-year budget rule, renewed each cycle (117.191 → 117.145). |
| ETHICS: Ethics Commission Website Changes (110.1) | FY 2021-22, FY 2023-24 → FY 2025-26 | The State Ethics Commission cannot change its public disclosure and accountability reporting system without approval from the House and Senate Ethics Committees — the filers keep approval power over the watchdog's disclosure tools. Not re-adopted for FY 2026-27. |
| ETHICS: Commission Meeting (110.2) | all six cycles | The Ethics Commission must meet at least monthly and post notice 24 hours ahead on its website. |
| SR: Prohibits Public Funded Lobbyists (118.6) | all six cycles | No state agency or institution may use general-fund money to employ or contract lobbyists; agencies certify compliance through the Ethics Commission. |
| GP: Prohibits Local Government Fund Public Funded Lobbyists | all six cycles | Counties, cities, and their associations may not use Local Government Fund money to pay employees for lobbying (117.95 → 117.92). |

## One-time and new items

| Fiscal year | Proviso | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-22 | 102.14 (ELECT: November 2020 Election Investigation Report) | Required the Election Commission to report the number of election-fraud investigations from the November 2020 election and post the report publicly. |
| 2024-25 | 45.11 (CU-PSA: Feasibility Study) | Funded a Clemson feasibility study for a Center for Civic Engagement. |
| 2026-27 | 117.219 (GP: Ethics Filing) — **new** | Officials who report income paid by the state budget on their statement of economic interests must name the specific government body that paid them — a piece of the H3570 disclosure bill that died in conference, carried out as a one-year rule. |
| 2026-27 | 118.21 (SR: Nonrecurring Revenue, USC line) | $2,500,000 for USC's Center for American Civic Leadership and Public Discourse — civic-education funding placed in the budget rather than a standalone law. |
