# Appendix C — Votes and support

Every floor passage/adoption-type roll call recorded for bills in the curated set, verbatim from the chamber vote histories. South Carolina never publishes committee vote tallies, so committee stops have no counts anywhere. Procedural motions (tabling, amendments, motions to continue) are excluded here; the full roll-call list per bill is in the source data. The failed 23–86 House vote on H3570 was a vote against concurring in Senate amendments — a version dispute, not a vote against the bill's own House version, which had passed 102–0.
