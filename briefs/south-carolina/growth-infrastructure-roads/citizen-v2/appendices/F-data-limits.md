# Appendix F — What this data can and cannot say

This appendix states the limits of the record behind the brief, so readers
know exactly how much weight each claim can carry.

## Where the data comes from

- Every bill, action, sponsor line, and floor vote comes from the South
  Carolina Legislature's official website (scstatehouse.gov), collected as
  part of a certified sweep of all 15,817 instruments filed in the 123rd
  through 126th General Assemblies (2019–2026), plus every chamber roll
  call and ratification.
- Budget provisos come from the enacted ("ratified") Part IB text of each
  annual General Appropriations Act on the same official site. Dollar
  figures quoted for provisos are verbatim from that text.
- The curated set was found by keyword discovery (official full-text search
  plus a local full-text and title scan of the certified universe) and then
  hand-reviewed bill by bill. It is a careful selection, not a
  proven-complete census of every growth-related bill. Nine bills were
  hand-added from the certified universe because the original search terms
  did not cover "sales tax", "annexation", or "hospitality tax" phrasings;
  a title scan for those phrasings was run across all four sessions.

## What the data cannot say

- **No committee vote counts exist.** South Carolina publishes committee
  *outcomes* ("Favorable", "Favorable with amendment") but never tally
  tables. Nothing in this packet states or implies a committee vote count,
  because none exists to cite.
- **No party labels.** Per-member ballot PDFs list names only, and this
  packet does not include the member-roster join that would attach parties.
  The brief therefore makes no party claims at all.
- **Voice votes leave no numbers.** Many bills — including some that became
  law — show few or no roll calls because South Carolina uses voice votes
  heavily. An empty vote history is a real answer, not missing data.
- **Committee silence has no recorded reason.** When a bill dies in its
  first committee, the record shows no hearing, no vote, and no explanation.
  The brief reports the stop; it cannot report why.
- **Whole-bill votes only for provisos.** Floor votes attach to the entire
  appropriations bill (or a named amendment), never to one proviso. No vote
  in this packet is presented as a vote on a specific proviso.
- **One-year rules.** A proviso lasts one budget year unless re-adopted.
  Statements like "renewed every year" describe the observed 2021–2026
  record, not a guarantee.
- **Background laws predate the record.** The 2017 gas-tax act (Act 40) and
  the 1999 Development Impact Fee Act are described as background; they were
  enacted before this record's 2019 start and are not part of the counts.
- **Dispositions are as of the collection date.** For non-enacted 126th
  session (2025–2026) bills, "did not pass" reflects the record as of
  2026-08-25, after the regular session's final regular calendar.
- **Local County Transportation Committee bills** routinely pass one chamber
  and finish through the county legislative delegation; their one-chamber
  passage is not a support signal for statewide policy and they are kept as
  context only.
- **The seven constituent proposals are Forum process input** from the
  Phase 2 Community Conversations (June–August 2026), labeled [P-…]. They
  record what participants proposed — not verified facts, and not the
  Forum's positions.
