# Appendix G — Roads and growth provisos in the state budget

South Carolina enacts one-year policy rules — provisos — inside Part IB of
each annual General Appropriations Act. A proviso lasts one budget year
unless re-adopted, and floor votes attach to the whole budget bill (or a
named amendment), never to a single proviso. The table below is the
hand-curated set of provisos that bear on roads, growth, and infrastructure
funding, selected from the full term-matched candidate sets (246–305
provisos per cycle). FY 2020-21 is a gap: the budget bill (H5201) died in
committee during COVID and the state ran on a continuing resolution, so
that year has no enacted Part IB.

## The recurring machinery

| Proviso (by caption) | Years present | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| CTC: Increased Funding (86.1) | all six enacted cycles | The 2017 gas-tax act's extra money for County Transportation Committees — the county boards that spend gas-tax "C" funds on local roads — must be spent exclusively on repairs, maintenance, and improvements to the state highway system. A renewed-every-year fix-it-first rule. |
| DOT: Project Priority List (84.9 → 84.8) | all six enacted cycles | SCDOT must publish its project priority lists — and the engineering directives explaining the ranking methodology — in a conspicuous, publicly accessible place on its website. |
| DOT: Preventative Maintenance Credit (84.12) | FY 2021-22 → FY 2024-25 | Authorized SCDOT to transfer part of the gas user fee to cover the 2017 act's preventative-maintenance tax credit for drivers. Stopped appearing in FY 2025-26. |
| DOT: Programmed Project Viewer Dashboard (84.18 → 84.16 → 84.15) | FY 2024-25 onward | Directs SCDOT to upgrade its public project dashboard: every project's status, forecast versus actual cost, projected completion date, and an on-time/on-budget list, with quarterly reports to the budget chairmen. |

## One-time money and new items

| Fiscal year | Proviso | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-22 | 117.96 (GP: School Construction Development Impact Fee Assessment Prohibition) | Barred local governments from charging development impact fees on new school construction, on pain of losing state aid. One year only; not renewed — the record's only enacted impact-fee rule. |
| 2021-22 | 118.18 (SR: Nonrecurring Revenue) | One-time money including $200,000,000 for the State Ports Authority's intermodal container transfer facility and waterborne cargo infrastructure. |
| 2022-23 | 118.19 (SR: Nonrecurring Revenue) | $133,636,230 for SCDOT Rural Interstate Funding. |
| 2024-25 | 118.22 (SR: Homestead Exemption Fund) | The record's largest one-year roads package, from a $600M fund sweep: $200,000,000 CTC Acceleration Fund; $100,000,000 Bridge Acceleration Fund; $117,401,000 Rural Road Safety Program. |
| 2025-26 | 118.22 (SR: Nonrecurring Revenue) | $200,000,000 for SCDOT Bridge Modernization. |
| 2026-27 | 118.21 (SR: Nonrecurring Revenue) | $175,000,000 CTC Acceleration; $50,000,000 Bridge Modernization. |
| 2026-27 | 84.18 (DOT: Road Buyback Program) — **new** | SCDOT must identify state roads that no longer serve a statewide purpose and negotiate transferring them to counties and cities, with buyback funds covering resurfacing and transition costs; report due February 28, 2027. |
