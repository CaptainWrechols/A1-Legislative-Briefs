# Appendix G — Cost-of-living provisos in the state budget

South Carolina enacts one-year policy rules — provisos — inside Part IB of
each annual General Appropriations Act. A proviso lasts one budget year
unless re-adopted, and floor votes attach to the whole budget bill (or a
named amendment), never to a single proviso. The tables below are the
hand-curated set of provisos that bear on utility oversight, financial
education, tax relief, and housing costs, selected from the full
term-matched candidate sets (434–564 provisos per cycle). FY 2020-21 is a
gap: the budget bill (H5201) died in committee during COVID and the state
ran on a continuing resolution, so that year has no enacted Part IB.

**Financial-education note (explicit none-found):** across all six enacted
cycles, exactly one proviso funds or directs personal-finance education —
FY 2022-23 proviso 1.101, the one-time order that created the graduation
requirement. No other cycle contains one.

## The recurring machinery

| Proviso (by caption) | Years present | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| GP: Homestead Exemption Fund | all six cycles | Suspends subsection (C) of the Homestead Exemption Fund statute (§11-11-156) for the year — a recurring budget-side adjustment to the fund that repays schools for the owner-occupied school-tax exemption. |
| GP: Personal Property Tax Relief Fund | all six cycles | Backstops counties that swapped vehicle/personal property taxes for a local sales tax: if the 2% sales tax falls short, the state Trust Fund for Tax Relief covers the gap. |
| SR: Tax Relief Reserve Fund | all six cycles | A reserve fund, separate from the General Fund, usable only for tax relief to businesses and individuals as provided by law. |
| PSC: Santee Cooper Billing | FY 2023-24 → FY 2026-27 | Lets the Public Service Commission bill Santee Cooper for the cost of overseeing it under Act 90 of 2021 — the oversight regime's funding lives in the budget, not permanent law. |
| DOR: Manufacturing Property Tax Reduction | FY 2023-24 → FY 2026-27 | Denies utilities (including solar farms) the manufacturing property tax reduction percentage, keeping utility property fully on local tax rolls. |
| GP: Tax Rate | FY 2025-26 → FY 2026-27 | Suspends the income tax law's phase-in trigger subsections (§12-6-510(B)(2) and (3)) so the rate table applies as written — the budget's lever for accelerating the income tax cut. |
| ORS: Energy Office | FY 2024-25 → FY 2026-27 | Authorizes the state Energy Office to administer federal IIJA/IRA programs (including household energy money) and carry funds forward. |

## Child care provisos (legislator-discussion topic, added 2026-09-04)

Verified against the full Part IB sets (`_universe/part1b/{year}/`), since
the issue's term-matched candidate files predate the childcare theme:

| Proviso (by caption) | Years present | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| DSS: Child Care Voucher | all six cycles (38.18 in FY 2021-22; 38.17 thereafter) | State funds used for child care vouchers must enroll recipients at providers exceeding minimum licensing standards (waivable case by case) — the annual rule governing the federal assistance program run through DSS. |
| DSS: Childcare Provider Fraud | FY 2026-27 (38.28, new) | DSS must assess and report on fraud identification among childcare providers taking state/federal funds or scholarship money. |
| SDE: Full-Day 4K / SDE-EIA: 4K & CERDEP provisos | all six cycles | The state pre-K program (Full-Day 4K / CERDEP) is administered and expanded through provisos each year — e.g. FY 2026-27 provisos 1.46, 1.102, 1A.20, 1A.25, 1A.43, 1A.48, 1A.71. |
| SDE: First Steps Transfer Plan / Division | FY 2023-24 (1.97), FY 2024-25 (1.109) | First Steps' move into the education department ran through provisos. |
