# Appendix G — Wage and workforce provisos in the state budget

South Carolina enacts one-year policy rules — provisos — inside Part IB of
each annual General Appropriations Act. A proviso lasts one budget year
unless re-adopted, and floor votes attach to the whole budget bill (or a
named amendment), never to a single proviso. The table below is the
hand-curated set of provisos that bear on wages and workforce pathways,
selected from the full term-matched candidate sets (566–674 provisos per
cycle). FY 2020-21 is a gap: the budget bill (H5201) died in committee
during COVID and the state ran on a continuing resolution, so that year has
no enacted Part IB.

## The recurring machinery

| Proviso (by caption) | Years present | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| GP: Lead Apprenticeship Agency | FY 2023-24 → FY 2026-27 | Makes the technical college board (through Apprenticeship Carolina) the lead agency for all registered apprenticeships in the state. Exists only as a budget rule, not permanent law. |
| TEC: Critical Statewide Workforce Needs | all six cycles | Directs technical college STEM/workforce money to fields with the most unmet employer demand, set with the Commerce and workforce agencies. |
| LEA lottery funding — SC WINS line | all six cycles | Lottery money funds SC Workforce Industry Needs Scholarships each year (amounts below). |
| GP: Employee Compensation | all six cycles | Sets the annual state-employee pay raise inside the budget. |
| SDE-EIA: Career Cluster Industry Partnerships | all six cycles | Grants to industry groups running certified career/technology education programs. |
| SDE-EIA: Career and Technology Education | all six cycles | Funds career and technology education in schools. |
| TEC: IDD Workforce Pilot | FY 2024-25 → FY 2026-27 | Pilot helping people with intellectual and developmental disabilities into the workforce. |
| TEC: SC Workforce Competitiveness Initiative | FY 2025-26 → FY 2026-27 | Public-awareness campaign on manufacturing and related careers. |
| TEC: Dual Enrollment Courses | FY 2025-26 → FY 2026-27 | Rules for technical college dual-enrollment courses. |
| SDE: SC Future Makers and Tallo | FY 2024-25 → FY 2026-27 | Platform connecting students to South Carolina employers. |
