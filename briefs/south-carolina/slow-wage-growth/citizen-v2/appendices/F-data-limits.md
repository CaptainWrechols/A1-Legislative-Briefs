# Appendix F — Data limits

What this dataset can and cannot say, in plain language.

1. **The set is keyword-discovered and hand-curated.** Pass 1 kept every hit
   from the State House full-text search plus a title/summary scan of the
   certified 2019–2026 universe (5,744 bills); a hand review kept 133 as the
   issue set (54 core, 52 adjacent, 27 context). A wage-related bill that
   used none of the search terms would be missed, though the term list is
   broad (26 terms).

2. **No committee vote counts exist — anywhere.** South Carolina publishes
   committee *outcomes* in bill histories ("Committee report: Favorable"),
   never tally tables. Nothing in this packet states or implies a committee
   vote count; any such number found elsewhere has no official source.

3. **No party labels.** Attaching parties requires joining ballot PDFs to a
   legislator roster, which was not fetched for this brief. The packet
   therefore makes no claims about which party supported or opposed
   anything.

4. **Floor counts are verbatim.** Every yes–no figure comes directly from
   the official chamber vote-history tables. Only passage-type votes
   (second/third reading, passage of bill) are used as support signals;
   tabling and amendment motions are procedural and excluded from the
   support tables.

5. **"Did not pass (session ended)" for 2025-26 bills** reflects the record
   as of the collection date (August 26, 2026), after the 126th General
   Assembly's regular calendar ended.

6. **Budget provisos:** FY 2020-21 has no enacted Part IB proviso set — the
   budget bill died in committee during COVID and the state ran on a
   continuing resolution — so proviso coverage runs FY 2021-22 through
   FY 2026-27. Proviso vote counts do not exist: floor votes attach to the
   whole appropriations bill, never one proviso.

7. **The four citizen proposals are Forum process input.** They come from
   Phase 2 Community Conversations (June–August 2026) and are labeled
   [P-…]; they are what participants proposed, not verified facts or
   Forum positions.

8. **No agency outcome data.** This packet maps the legislative record only.
   It does not measure whether enacted programs (WINS, Earn and Learn,
   apprenticeship credits) actually raised wages; that would need agency
   and labor-market data outside this dataset.
