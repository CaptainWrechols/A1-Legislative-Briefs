# Appendix H — Citizen proposals and the record

The four proposals below came out of the Forum's Phase 2 Community
Conversations (June–August 2026). They are **process input** — what
participants proposed, with the frequency and consensus noted at the events
— not verified facts or Forum positions. Each is matched against the
2019–2026 legislative record. A proposal with zero matched bills is a
finding, not a failure: it means lawmakers have not yet tried it.

## [P-raise-minimum-wage] Raise the minimum wage / livable wage

- **From the conversations:** raise the state minimum wage toward a livable
  wage. High frequency (3 events); mixed consensus; small-business cost
  concerns were the main counterpoint.
- **Matched bills (24):** 2019-20 — H3114, H3217, H3395, H3467, H4154, S147
  (constitutional amendment), S149; 2021-22 — H3018, H3184, H3341, H3480,
  H3675, S159, S343, S633 (ballot question), S634; 2023-24 — H3805, H5187,
  S216, S28 (ballot question), S291; 2025-26 — H3226, H3735 (wage boards),
  H3809.
- **Record:** none reported out of first committee; no recorded vote at any
  stage in eight years.
- **Near misses:** S533 (2022) and S1001 (2024) enacted wage floors for
  specific groups (disability subminimum ban; inmate private-industry
  floor).
- **Venue note:** the federal $7.25 floor applies; state law blocks local
  minimums, so the General Assembly is the only in-state venue.

## [P-age-bracketed-minimum-wage] Age-bracketed minimum wage with a separate youth wage

- **From the conversations:** a higher minimum for adult workers (18+) and a
  separate, lower youth wage for teens. High frequency (3 events); high
  consensus for a higher adult wage; the open questions were where to draw
  the age line and whether a youth wage displaces adult workers.
- **Matched bills:** none. No 2019–2026 bill proposes a youth wage, training
  wage, or any age-based bracket; "youth wage" and "training wage" return
  zero hits in every bill's full text.
- **Nearest record:** S533 (Act 209 of 2022) removed a subminimum tier
  (for workers with disabilities) rather than creating one.
- **Venue note:** federal law already permits a $4.25 youth training wage
  for a worker's first 90 days; any state bracket would sit on top of that
  and of a state minimum that does not yet exist.

## [P-workforce-development] Apprenticeships, career pathways, and technical training

- **From the conversations:** expand apprenticeships, career pathways, and
  technical-college training so workers can move into higher-paying jobs.
  Medium frequency (Greenville, Columbia); high consensus; funding and
  employer participation were the practical questions.
- **Matched bills (23) — the record's most successful lane.** Became law:
  H3144 (WINS scholarships, Act 204 of 2022), H4766 (workforce coordinating
  council, Act 194 of 2022), H3605 (Earn and Learn, Act 13 of 2023), H3726
  (Statewide Education and Workforce Development Act, Act 67 of 2023), S557
  (apprentice tax credit increase, Act 188 of 2024), H3863 (STEM Opportunity
  Act, Act 142 of 2026). Got support but didn't finish: H3576 (2019-20,
  House 105–0), H3759/S419 (2019-20 career-pathways overhaul, each passed
  its own chamber), H4060 (2023, House 108–2), S859 (2024, Senate 42–0),
  H3348 (2022, House 106–0). Died in first committee: H4022, S650, H3757,
  H3611, S461, H3197, H4984, H3225, H4600, H3479, S272.
- **Budget overlap:** the apprenticeship lead-agency role, WINS funding, and
  critical-workforce-needs funding live in one-year budget provisos
  (Appendix G).

## [P-employer-living-wage-incentives] Government incentives/bonuses for employers paying a living wage

- **From the conversations:** state incentives or bonuses for employers who
  pay a living wage. Medium frequency; mixed consensus; how to fund the
  incentives was disputed.
- **Matched bills (3):** H4603 (2026, Small Business Livable Wage Tax Credit
  Act — the direct precedent; died in House Ways and Means without a vote),
  H4245 (2025, credits for hiring and retaining people leaving public
  assistance; same committee, same result), S398 (2021, wage and job
  obligations on subsidy recipients; died in Senate Labor, Commerce and
  Industry).
- **Adjacent record:** wage-linked employer incentives already exist and
  pass when updated inside tax packages — job development credits (S901,
  Act 237 of 2022; H4087, Act 222 of 2024) and the apprentice credit (S557,
  Act 188 of 2024).
