# Appendix B — Theme scorecards

Each theme groups the curated bills by what they tried to do. The *history
basket* is a description of the past record only — "Often moved before,"
"Got support but didn't finish," or "Rarely moved before" — never a
prediction or a recommendation. *Certainty* reflects how many bills support
the pattern.

## Creating a state minimum wage

- **Bills:** 24 (all core) · **Became law:** 0 · **Basket:** Rarely moved before · **Certainty:** high
- **What was tried:** fixed hourly floors from $10 to $17; phased three-year schedules with automatic inflation adjustments; the recurring "SC Minimum Wage Act" (agency-adjusted floor); two resolutions for advisory ballot questions (2021 S633, 2023 S28); a constitutional amendment (2019 S147); and a wage-board mechanism letting the state workforce agency set pay rates by occupation (2025 H3735).
- **Where they stopped:** the first committee, every time. House Labor, Commerce and Industry took 8; House Ways and Means 5; Senate Labor, Commerce and Industry 8; Senate Judiciary 2 (the constitutional amendment and one ballot resolution); House Ways and Means also took the wage-board bill. None was reported out; none received a recorded vote at any stage in eight years.
- **Typical floor vote:** none exists.
- **Examples:** 2019-20 H3114 ($10.10), 2021-22 S159 (SC Minimum Wage Act), 2023-24 S28 (ballot question), 2025-26 H3735 (wage boards).

## Wage floors for specific groups

- **Bills:** 11 (4 core, 7 adjacent) · **Became law:** 2 · **Basket:** Often moved before (for narrow, targeted floors) · **Certainty:** medium
- **What passed:** 2021-22 S533 (Act 209 of 2022) banned subminimum pay for workers with disabilities and enacted the Employment First Initiative Act — Senate 43–0, House 104–4. 2023-24 S1001 (Act 192 of 2024) guaranteed inmates in private prison-industry programs at least the federal minimum wage — 45–0 and 109–0.
- **The path there:** the Employment First idea died quietly in 2019-20 (H4768, first committee), passed both chambers in 2021 (H3244, 106–9 and 44–0) and died in conference, then passed as S533 the next year.
- **Where the rest stopped:** general inmate-wage bills died in first committee in every session (2019-20 H5061, 2021-22 H4050, 2025-26 H3559 and H5357).
- **Examples:** 2021-22 S533, 2023-24 S1001, 2021-22 H3244, 2025-26 H5357.

## Apprenticeships and hiring tax credits

- **Bills:** 7 (all core) · **Became law:** 2 · **Basket:** Mixed — often moved as tax measures; unfinished as hiring-rule bills · **Certainty:** medium
- **What passed:** 2023-24 S557 (Act 188 of 2024) raised the apprentice income tax credit and extended how long employers can claim it — 42–0 and 111–0. 2023-24 H3605 (Act 13 of 2023), the Earn and Learn Act, connects students to registered apprenticeships for credit — 114–0 and 39–0.
- **Got support but didn't finish:** 2021-22 H3348 (credits for hiring formerly incarcerated people or veterans into apprenticeships) passed the House 106–0 and died in Senate Finance. 2023-24 S859 (skills-based hiring for state jobs) passed the Senate 42–0 and died in House Labor, Commerce and Industry.
- **Where the rest stopped:** the 2025-26 refiles of skills-based hiring (H3479, S272) and the credit-tightening bill (2023-24 H4600) died in first committee.

## Career training, technical colleges, and workforce programs

- **Bills:** 22 (16 core, 6 adjacent) · **Became law:** 5 · **Basket:** Often moved before · **Certainty:** high
- **What passed:** SC WINS workforce scholarships (2021-22 H3144, Act 204 of 2022, 105–1 and 40–0); the workforce coordinating council restructure (2021-22 H4766, Act 194 of 2022); the Statewide Education and Workforce Development Act creating a single state workforce office (2023-24 H3726, Act 67 of 2023, 108–5/102–3 and 41–2); the DEW cabinet-agency act (2023-24 H3783, Act 1 of 2023); and the STEM Opportunity Act (2025-26 H3863, Act 142 of 2026, 103–0 and 43–0).
- **Got support but didn't finish:** WINS 1.0 (2019-20 H3576) passed the House 105–0, got a favorable Senate committee report, and ran out of Senate calendar — the identical idea passed two years later. The career-pathways overhaul passed each chamber separately in 2019-20 (H3759, House 100–3; S419, Senate 40–4 after a long floor fight) and both died in the other chamber's committee when COVID ended the session. 2023-24 H4060 passed the House 108–2 and died in Senate Education. DEW restructuring passed the Senate twice (2023-24 S546, 42–0; 2025-26 S279, 41–0) and died in House committees.
- **Where the rest stopped:** first committee (data-oversight, dual-enrollment, workforce-diploma, service-year bills).

## Employer incentives tied to pay

- **Bills:** 11 (3 core, 8 adjacent) · **Became law:** 2 · **Basket:** Mixed — passes inside tax packages; stand-alone designs unproven · **Certainty:** medium
- **What passed:** job development credits — the state's existing wage-linked hiring incentive — were updated inside broader tax bills in 2021-22 (S901, Act 237 of 2022, 41–0 and 91–0) and 2023-24 (H4087, Act 222 of 2024, 45–0 and 84–12).
- **Not yet voted on:** 2025-26 H4603, the Small Business Livable Wage Tax Credit Act — the closest thing on record to the citizens' proposal — died in House Ways and Means without a vote, as did 2025-26 H4245 (credits for hiring people leaving public assistance). 2021-22 S398 (wage and job obligations on subsidy recipients, with clawbacks) died in Senate Labor, Commerce and Industry.
- **Got support but didn't finish:** 2021-22 H4252 (Redline Enterprise Zone Act) passed the House 103–2 and died in Senate Finance.

## Equal pay for equal work

- **Bills:** 11 (all adjacent) · **Became law:** 0 · **Basket:** Rarely moved before · **Certainty:** high
- **What was tried:** the SC Equal Pay for Equal Work Act and the Act to Establish Pay Equity, refiled each session; state-employee versions in three sessions.
- **Where they stopped:** first committee, all eleven, with no recorded vote.

## Take-home pay: overtime rules and taxes

- **Bills:** 6 (all adjacent) · **Became law:** 0 · **Basket:** Got support but didn't finish · **Certainty:** medium
- **The one that advanced:** 2025-26 H3368 (exempting overtime pay from state income tax) passed the House 121–0, was reported favorably by Senate Finance, then failed on the Senate floor 16–27 in March 2026 — the only losing floor vote in this record.
- **Where the rest stopped:** first committee (three sibling exemption bills; the daily-overtime mandate H4751).

## Other pay and job-rules bills

- **Bills:** 14 (all adjacent) · **Became law:** 2 · **Basket:** Mixed · **Certainty:** medium
- **What passed:** the military retirement income tax exemption, branded the Workforce Enhancement and Military Recognition Act (2021-22 H3247, Act 156 of 2022, 107–0 and 43–0), and consumer rules for earned-wage-access services (2023-24 S700, Act 190 of 2024).
- **Where the rest stopped:** paid sick leave (2021-22 H3469), ban-the-box for state hiring (2019-20 S549, 2023-24 S25), felon-reentry study committees (three sessions), and school support staff pay (2021-22 H4140, 2025-26 H3583) all died in first committee.
