This record holds 772 bills found for 2019–2025: 409 policy bills (healthcare cost, coverage, access, or provider-supply policy is a real part of the bill) and 363 context bills (found by broad search terms or omnibus indexing; kept so the search is auditable). Headline numbers in the front brief use the policy set only. "Where it ended" tells you the last step the bill reached — the record never says why it stopped.
