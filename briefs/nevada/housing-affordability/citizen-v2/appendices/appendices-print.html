1. Limit corporate/institutional ownership of housing - cap how many properties one owner can hold.

  • Status: Tried; stopped at the finish line
  • The record: The most-advanced idea in the set that is still not law. 2023 SB395 (Sen. Dina Neal, D) capped corporate/LLC purchases at 1,000 homes a year statewide, passed the Senate 14-6 and the Assembly 28-14, and was vetoed. 2025 SB391 cut the cap to 100 homes, passed the Senate 13-8, and died in the Assembly without a vote. In the 36th (2025) Special Session, SB10 (Sen. Cannizzaro) - a 1,000-unit cap plus a corporate-investor registry - passed the Senate 18-0 and then lost the Assembly final-passage vote 27-10: the bill’s registration-fee provisions required a two-thirds majority (28 of 42), so a 27-vote majority was recorded as Lost. What is law: 2025 AB457 orders an interim study of taxing corporate rental-property owners as single entities, reporting for 2027.
