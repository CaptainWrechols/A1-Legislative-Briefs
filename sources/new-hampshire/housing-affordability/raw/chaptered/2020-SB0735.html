STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE

 

In the Year of Our Lord Two Thousand Twenty

 

AN ACT repealing the housing appeals board.

 

Be it Enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court convened:

 

1  Repeal.  RSA 679, relative to the housing appeals board, is repealed.

2  Salary.  Amend RSA 94:1-a, I(b) as follows:

Delete:

DD housing appeals board member

DD housing appeals board chair

3  Lapse of Appropriation to General Fund.  On the effective date of this section, any balance remaining in the appropriation to 2019, 346:262 for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2021, shall lapse to the general fund.

4  Effective Date.  This act shall take effect July 1, 2020, at 12:01 a.m.
