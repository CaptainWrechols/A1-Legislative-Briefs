## Existing machinery (what any new design would build on)

- **Two childcare tax credits already in statute:** the employer program
  credit (§12-6-3440) and the individual child & dependent care credit
  (§12-6-3380). Every filed assistance design extends this machinery.
- **The assistance program itself is federal money run through DSS** (the SC
  child care scholarship/voucher program), regulated annually by proviso:
  DSS: Child Care Voucher (38.18 in FY 2021-22, 38.17 in every cycle since —
  vouchers must be used at providers exceeding minimum licensing standards)
  and, new in FY 2026-27, DSS: Childcare Provider Fraud (38.28).
- **State pre-K expands through the budget, not statute:** Full-Day 4K /
  CERDEP provisos in every enacted cycle; First Steps' move into the
  education department ran through provisos (1.97 FY 2023-24, 1.109
  FY 2024-25).

## What the record indicates (descriptive, basket language)

1. **Same shape as this issue's other consumer lanes.** Regulation moves
   (three acts, plus S946's unanimous double passage); direct assistance
   sits in House Ways and Means / Senate Finance without votes — the same
   two rooms holding the homestead and vehicle-tax bills.
2. **Signals that distinguish it from the zero-movement lanes:** filings are
   accelerating (three assistance designs in the 126th, one filed August
   2026); every design uses the tax code — the venue where this record's
   enacted cost relief actually runs (the 2022 and 2026 income tax cuts, the
   S866 sales-tax swap); S946 shows childcare bills pass unanimously when
   they reach a floor; and both expansion and conditions designs are filing
   at once — an active, unsettled lane, not a dead one.
3. **Never filed:** a direct-payment (non-tax-code) state subsidy to
   families or providers, and a state-funded expansion of assistance beyond
   the federal voucher money. The stipend design (H4394) has been filed once
   and never heard.
4. **Committee-tally caveat:** as everywhere in this record, the committee
   stops above have no vote counts — South Carolina does not publish them —
   so "never voted on" means no recorded vote exists at any stage.
