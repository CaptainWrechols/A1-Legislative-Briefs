# HB2 (budget policy trailer) — energy sections, 2021 / 2023 / 2025

New Hampshire passes its two-year budget in two bills: HB1 (the money)
and HB2, a policy 'trailer' that bundles dozens to hundreds of legal
changes into one bill. The state's biggest energy-governance change of
this period — the creation of the Department of Energy and the
restructuring of the Public Utilities Commission — was made inside the
2021 trailer, not as a standalone bill; the 2023 trailer set the EV
registration surcharge and the regional energy advocacy fund; the 2025
trailer swept and permanently diverted the renewable energy fund.
This file is the hand-reviewed, section-level energy analysis for the
three budget cycles in scope.

**Votes are on the whole trailer.** Every roll call below was cast on
HB2 as a package. A lawmaker's vote on HB2 says nothing certain about
any single section.

## HB2 2021 — Laws of 2021, Chapter 91

*468 sections extracted; 97 energy-relevant after review; 42 floor roll calls on the whole bill.*

Final passage (whole bill): Senate 14–10 (Conference Committee Report, 2021-06-24); House 198–181 (Adopt Conference Committee Report, 2021-06-24).

### Core energy sections

- **91:98 — Issuance of Electric Rate Reduction Bonds; 2027.** Extended electric rate reduction bond authority to 2027 (RSA 369-B:5): the securitization tool that let utilities refinance stranded costs - the statute later used for storm-cost and divestiture financing that ratepayers repay on their bills. *(affects RSA 369-B:5, RSA 77)*
- **91:187 — New Chapter; Department of Energy.** Created the Department of Energy (new RSA 12-P): a cabinet agency absorbing most Public Utilities Commission staff and functions plus the old Office of Strategic Initiatives' energy programs - the biggest restructuring of New Hampshire energy regulation since electric restructuring, moving policy, ratepayer advocacy support, and program administration out of the utility tribunal. *(affects RSA 12-P:11, RSA 374-F:10, RSA 541-A, RSA 94:1-a)*
- **91:204 — Public Utilities Commission; Commission; Term.** Rewrote the Public Utilities Commission itself: three full-time commissioners on staggered six-year terms, nominated through a screening process - the leaner adjudicative tribunal left after the department split. *(affects RSA 21-G:10, RSA 21-G:9, RSA 363:1)*
- **91:206 — Public Utilities Commission; Prohibition on Future Employment.** Barred PUC commissioners from taking employment with any utility the commission regulates for one year after leaving office - a revolving-door restriction on the state's utility regulators. *(affects RSA 363:12-b)*
- **91:211 — Office of the Consumer Advocate; Attachment to Department of Energy.** Kept the Office of the Consumer Advocate - the ratepayers' lawyer in utility cases - independent, administratively attached to the new Department of Energy rather than the commission. *(affects RSA 21-G:10, RSA 363:28)*
- **91:227 — Site Evaluation Committee; Membership; Administrative Attachment.** Restructured the Site Evaluation Committee - the body that approves or rejects large energy facilities like transmission lines and wind plants - shrinking it and attaching it administratively to the new department. *(affects RSA 162-H:3, RSA 21-G:10)*
- **91:228 — Committee Established.** Created a study committee on further revisions to the Site Evaluation Committee - the opening move in the siting-reform fight that continued all period.
- **91:234 — Net Energy Metering.** Rewrote the group-host net metering rules (RSA 362-A:9, XIV): how a customer-generator hosts a group of customers and how hosts register and report - the mechanics that community solar projects run on. *(affects RSA 362-A:9, RSA 362-F:2)*
- **91:240 — Electric Renewable Portfolio Standard; Renewable Energy Fund.** Rewrote the renewable energy fund statute (RSA 362-F:10): the alternative-compliance-payment money that funds rebates and low-income solar programs is continually appropriated to the new department. *(affects RSA 362-A:9, RSA 362-F:10, RSA 362-F:3, RSA 362-F:8)*
- **91:242 — Expenses of the Public Utilities Commission Against Certain Utilities.** Rewrote how the state charges utilities for their own regulation: the expenses of the commission and the new department are assessed against the utilities they regulate. *(affects RSA 362, RSA 363-A:1, RSA 365:37, RSA 365:38)*
- **91:243 — Expenses of Public Utilities Commission Against Certain Utilities; Assessment.** The assessment formula itself: each public utility is assessed its share of regulatory costs based on gross revenue from New Hampshire customers - the funding backbone for both agencies, which flows into rates. *(affects RSA 363-A:2)*
- **91:281 — Restructuring Policy Principles; Implementation.** Amended the electric utility restructuring policy principles (RSA 374-F:3) and their implementation - the ground rules of New Hampshire's competitive electricity market, including how energy efficiency programs and the system benefits charge are administered. *(affects RSA 362-A, RSA 362-F, RSA 362-F:10, RSA 362-F:2)*
- **91:282 — Electric Utility Restructuring; Ratepayer Protection.** Rewrote the restructuring chapter's ratepayer-protection provisions - who watches that restructuring savings reach customers. *(affects RSA 374-F:4-b, RSA 541-A:29, RSA 541-A:29-a)*
- **91:285 — Offshore Wind and Port Development; Commission Established; Membership.** Moved the offshore wind and port development commission under the new department's leadership - the state's standing body on Gulf of Maine offshore wind. *(affects RSA 374-F:10)*
- **91:288 — Investigation of Energy Storage.** Turned the energy storage docket into a department investigation: how storage projects get paid for avoided transmission and distribution costs - the grid-modernization question behind peak-demand costs. *(affects RSA 374-F, RSA 374-G, RSA 374-H:2)*
- **91:292 — Multi-use Data Energy Platform.** Rewrote the multi-use energy data platform statute (RSA 378:50-52): the shared platform giving customers and competitive suppliers access to electric and gas usage data - the infrastructure that time-of-use rates and smart-meter programs depend on. *(affects RSA 363:28, RSA 363:37, RSA 363:38, RSA 374:62)*
- **91:293 — Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative; Energy Efficiency Fund and Use of Auction Proceeds.** Moved the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative energy efficiency fund to the department and re-enacted how RGGI auction proceeds are used - most consumer proceeds rebated on electric bills, with a set-aside funding low-income energy efficiency programs. *(affects RSA 125-O:23)*

### Core energy sections

- **79:110 — Electric Utility Restructuring; Implementation.** Amended electric utility restructuring implementation (RSA 374-F:4): how the system benefits charge's energy efficiency component is set and reviewed - the ratepayer charge that funds the NHSaves efficiency programs. *(affects RSA 374-F:4)*

- **79:581 — Department of Energy; Study on Graduated Public Assistance Mandated.** Ordered the Department of Energy to study a graduated, proportional low-income home energy assistance program for households that earn too much for existing fuel assistance - the benefits-cliff study on heating help.
