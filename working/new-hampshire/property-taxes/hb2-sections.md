# HB2 (budget policy trailer) — tax and revenue sections, 2021 / 2023 / 2025

New Hampshire passes its two-year budget in two bills: HB1 (the money)
and HB2, a policy 'trailer' that bundles dozens to hundreds of legal
changes into one bill. The state's biggest tax changes of this period —
the business tax rate cuts, the interest-and-dividends repeal, the
education funding rewrites, and the fate of municipal revenue sharing —
were all made inside HB2, not as standalone bills. This file is the
hand-reviewed, section-level tax/revenue analysis for the three budget
cycles in scope.

**Votes are on the whole trailer.** Every roll call below was cast on
HB2 as a package. A lawmaker's vote on HB2 says nothing certain about
any single section.

## HB2 2021 — Laws of 2021, Chapter 91

*468 sections extracted; 29 tax/revenue-relevant after review; 42 floor roll calls on the whole bill.*

Final passage (whole bill): Senate 14–10 (Conference Committee Report, 2021-06-24); House 198–181 (Adopt Conference Committee Report, 2021-06-24).

### Core tax and revenue sections

- **91:48 — Revenue Sharing; Suspension.** Suspended revenue sharing with cities and towns (RSA 31-A) for another biennium - the general-fund aid stream that had been suspended in every budget since 2010. *(affects RSA 31-A)*
- **91:89 — Rate.** Began phasing out the interest and dividends tax: the 5 percent rate drops to 4 percent in 2023, 3 percent in 2024, 2 percent in 2025, and 1 percent in 2026 - the schedule the full repeal rides on. *(affects RSA 77:1)* *(hand-added; missed by term matching)*
- **91:99 — Repeals; Interest and Dividends Taxation; 2027.** Repealed the interest and dividends tax (RSA 77) outright, effective January 1, 2027 - the end point of the phase-out schedule. (The 2023 trailer later moved this date up to 2025.) *(affects RSA 195-H:10, RSA 195-K:4, RSA 21-J:45, RSA 261:52-a)*
- **91:103 — Imposition of Tax.** Cut the meals and rooms tax rate from 9 percent to 8.5 percent, effective October 2021 - the tax's first rate cut; its revenue feeds the general fund, the education trust fund, and the towns' share. *(affects RSA 78-A:6)* *(hand-added; missed by term matching)*
- **91:109 — Business Enterprise Tax; Rate Reduced.** Cut the business enterprise tax rate from 0.675 percent to 0.55 percent for tax periods ending on or after December 31, 2022, and repealed the earlier revenue-trigger contingencies. *(affects RSA 21-I:8, RSA 77-E:2)*
- **91:110 — Business Profits Tax; Rate Reduced; Contingency Deleted.** Cut the business profits tax rate from 7.7 percent to 7.6 percent for tax periods ending on or after December 31, 2022, deleting the contingency that could have raised it back to 7.9 percent. *(affects RSA 21-I:8, RSA 77-A:2)*
- **91:112 — Meals and Rooms Tax; Disposition of Revenue; Fund Established.** Rewrote how meals and rooms tax money is shared: created the dedicated meals and rooms municipal revenue fund, with the towns' share (after administration and the education trust fund's cut) distributed to every city and town by population - the change that pushed the local share toward the statutory 30 percent. *(affects RSA 198:15-a, RSA 198:39, RSA 78-A:26, RSA 78-A:6)*
- **91:320 — Appropriation; Department of Education.** Appropriated $30 million from the education trust fund for school building aid on new projects - construction costs that otherwise land on local property taxes. *(affects RSA 198:15-a)*
- **91:322 — Education Tax Revenue; Fiscal Year 2023.** Cut the statewide education property tax (SWEPT) for fiscal year 2023: the commissioner sets the rate to raise $263 million instead of the usual $363 million target - a one-year, $100 million property-tax reduction. *(affects RSA 76:3, RSA 76:8, RSA 82, RSA 83-F)*
- **91:323 — Supplemental Education Payment; Fiscal Year 2023.** Guaranteed that no municipality's total education grant would fall because of the SWEPT cut: the state makes up any decrease with a supplemental payment. *(affects RSA 198:41, RSA 198:42)*

### Adjacent (tax-administration and funding-mechanics) sections

- **91:26 — County Reimbursement of Funds; Limitations on Payments.** Capped how fast county payments to the state for long-term care can grow (2 percent a year for the biennium) - the reimbursement stream counties raise through county property taxes. *(affects RSA 167:18-a, RSA 169-B, RSA 541-A)*
- **91:51 — Certain Differential Aid Calculations; Fiscal Year 2022.** Let districts use their higher pre-pandemic or pandemic-year enrollment count for fiscal 2022 aid, so pandemic enrollment drops would not cut adequacy grants. *(affects RSA 198:38, RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-e)*
- **91:55 — Transfer; Education Trust Fund.** Moved $35 million of education trust fund surplus into a restricted account to fund additional school relief aid in fiscal years 2022 and 2023. *(affects RSA 198:40-e)*
- **91:60 — Education Trust Fund Created and Invested.** Rewrote what the education trust fund may pay for - adequacy grants, the low and moderate income homeowners' property tax relief program, and the new education freedom accounts among them. *(affects RSA 186-C:18, RSA 188-E:9, RSA 198:15-b, RSA 198:39)*
- **91:61 — Transfer; Education Trust Fund.** Transferred $1 million a year from the education trust fund to the public school infrastructure fund in each year of the biennium. *(affects RSA 198:15-y:)*
- **91:69 — Distribution of Meals and Rooms Tax; Division of Travel and Tourism Development.** Suspended the earmark that sends a slice of meals and rooms revenue to the state's travel and tourism division for the biennium. *(affects RSA 12-O:11-b)*
- **91:74 — State Aid Grants; Department of Environmental Services.** Froze new state aid grants for local water, wastewater, and landfill infrastructure projects for the biennium (existing commitments kept) - deferring a municipal aid stream. *(affects RSA 149-M, RSA 486, RSA 486-A)*
- **91:87 — Revenue Stabilization Account; Cap.** Raised the cap on the rainy day fund (revenue stabilization reserve) to 10 percent of general fund revenue, letting the state bank more surplus. *(affects RSA 7:6-e, RSA 9:13-e)*
- **91:106 — Business Enterprise Tax; Returns.** Raised the business enterprise tax filing thresholds to $250,000 of receipts or tax base, dropping the smallest businesses out of the filing pool. *(affects RSA 21-J:39, RSA 77-E:1, RSA 77-E:5)*
- **91:115 — Business Profits Tax; Credit Carry-forward Limited; Payments Due With Returns and Estimates.** Limited how much overpaid business profits tax can be carried forward as a credit (to 500 percent of the current year's tax), with the rest refunded. *(affects RSA 21-J:28-a, RSA 77-A:6, RSA 77-A:7)*
- **91:116 — Business Enterprise Tax; Credit Carry-forward Limited; Payments Due With Returns.** Applied the same 500 percent carry-forward limit and refund rule to business enterprise tax overpayments. *(affects RSA 21-J:28-a, RSA 77-E:5, RSA 77-E:6)*
- **91:117 — New Section; Commission to Study Limiting the Business Tax Credit Carry Over.** Created a commission to study limiting the business tax credit carry-over - including whether unused credit refunds could be redirected to other uses. *(affects RSA 77-A)*
- **91:295 — Tax Expenditure Report; Weighted Apportionment Factors Removed.** Updated the annual tax expenditure report statute - the report that tells lawmakers what each tax credit and exemption costs in forgone revenue. *(affects RSA 162-L:10, RSA 162-N:6, RSA 188-E:9-a, RSA 71-C:2)*
- **91:313 — School Building Aid; Annual Grant for Leased Space.** Extended annual school building aid lease grants to more kinds of district lease arrangements. *(affects RSA 194-B:3-a, RSA 195-A:1, RSA 195:1, RSA 198:15-hh)*
- **91:315 — New Paragraph; Kindergarten Adequate Education Grants.** Made full-day kindergarten adequacy grants a standing charge on the education trust fund every year, ending the keno-revenue-dependent formula. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 198:48-b)*
- **91:316 — Appropriation; Department of Education.** Appropriated $1.9 million to top up fiscal 2021 kindergarten and adequacy grant payments. *(affects RSA 198:48-b)*
- **91:332 — American Rescue Plan Act; Unanticipated Revenue.** Let municipalities accept and spend federal American Rescue Plan money as 'unanticipated revenue' without a town-meeting vote. *(affects RSA 31:95-b)*
- **91:431 — New Chapter; Education Freedom Accounts.** Created the education freedom account (EFA) program - per-pupil adequacy money following students out of public schools, paid from the education trust fund; the fund's biggest new draw. *(affects RSA 193-A:5, RSA 193-C:6, RSA 193:1, RSA 194-F)*
- **91:469 — New Section; Business Enterprise Tax; Granite State Paid Family Leave Plan Tax Credit.** Created a business enterprise tax credit worth 50 percent of premiums an employer pays into the new Granite State paid family leave plan. *(affects RSA 21-I:100, RSA 77-E)*

## HB2 2023 — Laws of 2023, Chapter 79

*612 sections extracted; 25 tax/revenue-relevant after review; 17 floor roll calls on the whole bill.*

Final passage (whole bill): House 326–53 (Concur (with Senate changes), 2023-06-08).

### Core tax and revenue sections

- **79:83 — Appropriation; Public School Infrastructure Fund.** Put $10 million from the education trust fund into the public school infrastructure fund for school construction and safety projects. *(affects RSA 198:15-y)*
- **79:85 — Taxation of Incomes; Rate.** Rewrote the interest and dividends tax schedule to end at 3 percent in 2024: the previous 2 percent (2025) and 1 percent (2026) steps were deleted so the tax stops a full two years early. *(affects RSA 77:1)*
- **79:86 — Returns for Interest and Dividends Taxes; 2027.** Moved the final interest-and-dividends return filing rules from 2027 to 2025 to match the accelerated repeal. *(affects RSA 541-A, RSA 77)*
- **79:88 — Amend Effective Date; Amend Repeal of Interest and Dividends Tax from 2027 to 2025.** Changed the repeal's effective date: the interest and dividends tax now ends January 1, 2025 instead of 2027.
- **79:109 — Revenue Sharing; Suspension.** Suspended revenue sharing with cities and towns (RSA 31-A) for the 2024-2025 biennium - again. *(affects RSA 31-A)*
- **79:150 — Cost of an Opportunity for an Adequate Education.** Rewrote the cost of an adequate education: base per-pupil aid rises to $4,100 with larger add-ons for free-and-reduced-lunch, special education, and English learners - the state share that offsets local school property taxes. *(affects RSA 193-C:3, RSA 193-C:6, RSA 193-E:2-a, RSA 198:40-a)*
- **79:153 — Extraordinary Need Grants; 2023.** Rewrote extraordinary need grants for 2023 - extra aid scaled to a town's equalized property value per pupil, targeting the towns with the weakest tax bases. *(affects RSA 1, RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-f, RSA 198:42)*
- **79:154 — Extraordinary Need Grants; 2025.** Set the 2025-and-after version of extraordinary need grants, extending the property-poor-town aid formula. *(affects RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-f)*
- **79:156 — Determination of Education Grants.** Rewrote how each municipality's total education grant is determined, including hold-harmless rules so no town's grant falls below prior levels while the new formula phases in. *(affects RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-e, RSA 198:40-f, RSA 198:41)*
- **79:191 — Business Profits Tax; Distribution to Education Trust Fund.** Fixed the education trust fund's share of the business profits tax at 41 percent of revenue - replacing the old 'increase attributable to' formula with a straight statutory split. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 77-A:2, RSA 77-A:20-a)*
- **79:192 — Business Enterprise Tax; Distribution to Education Trust Fund.** Fixed the education trust fund's share of the business enterprise tax at 41 percent of revenue, the same straight split. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 77-E:14, RSA 77-E:2)*

### Adjacent (tax-administration and funding-mechanics) sections

- **79:67 — Board of Tax and Land Appeals.** Restated the Board of Tax and Land Appeals as a 3-member body of tax-law experts - the state board that hears property tax abatement appeals. *(affects RSA 71-B:1)*
- **79:137 — Education Trust Fund Appropriations Reductions; Education Freedom Accounts.** Reduced the education trust fund appropriation earmarked for education freedom accounts by $10 million in each year of the biennium, an accounting true-up to actual enrollment.
- **79:138 — Education Trust Fund Created and Invested.** Re-enacted the education trust fund statute, restating everything the fund may pay for - adequacy grants, homeowners' property tax relief, EFAs, court-ordered placements, and school building aid among them. *(affects RSA 186-C:18, RSA 186-C:19-b, RSA 188-E:9, RSA 193-C)*
- **79:141 — Special Education; State Aid.** Changed special education state aid so districts are reimbursed for costs above 3.5 times the state average per-pupil cost - catastrophic costs that otherwise hit local budgets. *(affects RSA 186-C:18, RSA 186-C:19-b)*
- **79:143 — Liability for Children with Disabilities in Certain Court Ordered Placements.** Shifted liability for certain court-ordered placements of children with disabilities, defining when the state rather than the school district pays. *(affects RSA 169-B, RSA 169-B:13, RSA 169-B:40, RSA 186-)*
- **79:144 — Appropriation; Court Ordered Placements.** Appropriated $9.2 million from the education trust fund for court-ordered placement costs. *(affects RSA 186-C:19-b, RSA 198:39)*
- **79:147 — Department of Education; Appropriation; CTE Renovation Projects; Sugar River Valley Regional Technical Center.** Appropriated $12.5 million from the education trust fund for the Sugar River Valley career and technical education renovation project. *(affects RSA 198:39)*
- **79:148 — Appropriation.** Appropriated $7.6 million from the education trust fund for the Winnisquam regional CTE renovation project.
- **79:248 — Tax on Meals and Rooms; Population Figures.** Updated the population-estimate process that determines each town's share of the meals and rooms tax distribution. *(affects RSA 78-A:25)*
- **79:264 — Appropriation; Department of Education; Kindergarten Adequate Education Grants.** Appropriated $315,700 from the education trust fund for new full-day kindergarten adequacy grants. *(affects RSA 198:48-b)*
- **79:266 — Assessing Certification Board; Rulemaking and Implementation.** Converted the assessing certification board into an advisory board inside the office of professional licensure - the body that certifies the assessors who set property values (companion sections 79:267-269 carry the details). *(affects RSA 310-C)*
- **79:349 — Revenue Information Management System Account.** Extended the Department of Revenue Administration's revenue information management system account - the tax-collection IT system funded from the extra revenue it captures. *(affects RSA 21-J:1-b)*
- **79:512 — Department of Environmental Services; Appropriation.** Appropriated $15 million in each of fiscal 2026 and 2027 for payments to communities for wastewater projects - state aid the 2025 trailer would later repeal before it was paid. *(affects RSA 486)*
- **79:583 — New Section; Study Commission Established.** Created a study commission on the recent charitable gaming law changes, including the newly authorized historic horse racing machines - the fastest-growing state gaming revenue stream. *(affects RSA 284)*

## HB2 2025 — Laws of 2025, Chapter 141

*461 sections extracted; 32 tax/revenue-relevant after review; 45 floor roll calls on the whole bill.*

Final passage (whole bill): Senate 16–8 (Conference Committee Report, 2025-06-26); House 184–183 (Adopt Conference Committee Report, 2025-06-26).

### Core tax and revenue sections

- **141:26 — New Chapter; Video Lottery Terminals.** Legalized video lottery terminals (slot-machine-style VLTs) at licensed charitable gaming facilities: the state collects 31 percent of gross machine revenue, split 75 percent to the general fund and 25 percent to the lottery's special fund - the budget's biggest new revenue source. *(affects RSA 284:21-j, RSA 284:22-b, RSA 287-D, RSA 287-D:11)*
- **141:80 — Education; School Money; Education Trust Fund Created and Invested.** Capped the education trust fund's balance: any surplus over $20 million at the close of a biennium is swept into the general fund. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 21-I:8)*
- **141:111 — Repeal.** Repealed RSA 31-A, revenue sharing with cities and towns, outright - the municipal aid statute suspended in every budget since 2010 is now off the books entirely. *(affects RSA 31-A)*
- **141:112 — Repeal.** Repealed the 2023 budget's $30 million in wastewater state aid payments to communities before the money went out.
- **141:132 — Business Profits Tax; Distribution of Funds.** Cut the education trust fund's share of the business profits tax from 41 percent to 39 percent, keeping more business tax revenue in the general fund. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 77-A:2, RSA 77-A:20-a)*
- **141:133 — Business Enterprise Tax; Distribution of Funds.** Cut the education trust fund's share of the business enterprise tax from 41 percent to 39 percent. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 77-E:14, RSA 77-E:2)*
- **141:134 — Tobacco Tax; Distribution of Funds.** Rewrote the tobacco tax split: 39 percent of tobacco revenue to the education trust fund, the rest to the general fund. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 78:2, RSA 78:24)*
- **141:135 — Tax on Transfer of Real Property; Distribution of Funds.** Rewrote the real estate transfer tax split: 39 percent to the education trust fund, the rest to the general fund. *(affects RSA 198:39, RSA 78-B:1, RSA 78-B:13)*
- **141:223 — Education; Adequate Education; Education Trust Fund; Annual Adjustment.** Delayed the annual 2 percent inflation adjustment of per-pupil adequacy aid to July 2026 - a pause in the automatic growth of state school aid. *(affects RSA 194-B:11, RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-d, RSA 198:40-f)*
- **141:224 — Education; Adequate Education; Education Trust Fund; Extraordinary Need Grants.** Re-enacted the extraordinary need grant formula - the extra aid tied to a town's equalized property value per pupil. *(affects RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-f)*
- **141:225 — New Section; Education; Adequate Education; Education Trust Fund; Fiscal Capacity Disparity Aid.** Created fiscal capacity disparity aid: up to $1,250 extra per pupil for municipalities with less than $1 million of equalized property value per pupil, phasing out by $1.6 million - aid aimed squarely at property-poor towns. *(affects RSA 198, RSA 198:40-a)*
- **141:226 — Education; Adequate Education; Education Trust Fund; Determination of Education Grants.** Rewrote how each municipality's education grant is computed to fold in the new fiscal capacity disparity aid. *(affects RSA 198:40-a, RSA 198:40-f, RSA 198:40-g, RSA 198:41)*
- **141:358 — Tax Amnesty.** Created a tax amnesty program: back taxes paid during the amnesty window owe no penalties and only half the usual interest - a one-time revenue catch-up. *(affects RSA 21-J:28)*
- **141:389 — Legislative Declaration of Authority Regarding Public Education.** Declared the legislature's position on the Claremont school-funding rulings - asserting that defining and funding an adequate education is the legislature's prerogative, while the statewide education property tax and adequacy formula remain under active court challenge.

### Adjacent (tax-administration and funding-mechanics) sections

- **141:79 — Education; School Money; Education Trust Fund Created and Invested.** Amended the education trust fund's purpose list, including how court-ordered placement costs are paid. *(affects RSA 186-C:19-b, RSA 193:27, RSA 198:15-hh, RSA 198:15-y)*
- **141:83 — Determination of Education Grants.** Moved the final determination of education grants to October 1 and guaranteed each municipality at least 95 percent of its estimated grant. *(affects RSA 198:41, RSA 198:42)*
- **141:139 — New Paragraph; Revenue Stabilization Reserve Account.** Authorized drawing down the rainy day fund to cover a fiscal 2025 general-fund deficit, if the audit finds one. *(affects RSA 21-I:8, RSA 9:13-e)*
- **141:187 — New Paragraph; Taxation; Business Profits Tax; Credits.** Created a 'granite patron of the arts' business profits tax credit for half of donations to the new state arts fund. *(affects RSA 541-A, RSA 77-A:5)*
- **141:188 — New Section; Business Enterprise Tax; Granite Patron of the Arts Credit.** Let unused granite patron of the arts credits apply against the business enterprise tax. *(affects RSA 77-A:5, RSA 77-E)*
- **141:276 — Education; Special Education; State Aid.** Restructured special education state aid administration and reimbursement - the catastrophic-cost aid that shields local budgets. *(affects RSA 186-C:18, RSA 186-C:19-b)*
- **141:278 — Education; Special Education; State Aid.** Rewrote the funding rule for statewide special education programs. *(affects RSA 186-C:18, RSA 198:39)*
- **141:320 — Dedicated Funds; Amendments, Lapses, and Other Uses.** Ordered the governor to raise general fund revenues or cut appropriations by a combined $16 million per year for the biennium, on top of the budget's line items. *(affects RSA 541-A, RSA 6:12)*

- **141:340 — Board of Tax and Land Appeals; Quorum.** Adjusted the Board of Tax and Land Appeals quorum rules - the board that hears property tax appeals, now also backstopping the housing appeals board. *(affects RSA 71-B:6)*
